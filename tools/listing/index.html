    Where the rules come from. Amazon’s product title requirements (200 characters for most categories, no ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless in the brand, no word more than twice except small words), its guide to search terms (under 250 bytes, no brand, punctuation or subjective words) and its restricted products rules on pesticide, drug and FDA claims. Seller Central help needs a login, and every category has its own style guide, so this doesn’t cover every rule.

    Some checks are estimates. The 255-character bullet and 2,000-character description limits are the usual figures, and some categories allow more. The ALL-CAPS check, the lists of promotional, medical, pesticide and guarantee phrases, and counting only words with a letter for the repeated-word rule are our own, since Amazon reviews these by judgement.
